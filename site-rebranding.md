\# Project: AIwant.ro Strategic Rebranding \& Conversion Optimization



\## 1. Context \& Objective

The current website functions as a technical portfolio (Vendor approach). We need to transform it into a \*\*Strategic Partner approach\*\*. The goal is to stop selling "websites" and start selling "business efficiency, scalability, and ROI through automation and custom platforms."



\*\*Target Audience:\*\* Business owners, Operations Managers, and CEOs looking to reduce operational costs and increase revenue through digitalization.



\## 2. Core Messaging Strategy (The Shift)

\*   \*\*Old Message:\*\* "We build websites and automation tools."

\*   \*\*New Message:\*\* "Transforming manual chaos into automated growth. We build the digital infrastructure your business needs to scale."



\## 3. Structural Changes Required



\### A. Hero Section (The Hook)

\- \*\*Headline:\*\* Focus on high-level business impact. (e.g., "Scale Your Operations with Intelligent Automation \& Custom Digital Platforms.")

\- \*\*Sub-headline:\*\* Explain the mechanism. (e.g., "We eliminate manual bottlenecks and build high-performance digital ecosystems that drive efficiency and revenue.")

\- \*\*Primary CTA:\*\* "Book a Free Digitalization Audit"

\- \*\*Secondary CTA:\*\* "Explore Our Solutions"



\### B. The "Problem" Section (New Component)

Introduce a section titled "Is your business held back by manual processes?" 

List 3 pain points:

1\. \*\*Time Leaks:\*\* Valuable hours lost to repetitive, manual data entry.

2\. \*\*Human Error:\*\* Costly mistakes caused by fragmented workflows.

3\. \*\*Scaling Barriers:\*\* Inability to grow because your current systems can't handle more volume.



\### C. Service Rebranding (The Solution)

Rename and re-describe services:

\- \*\*From "Web Development"\*\* $\\rightarrow$ \*\*"High-Conversion Digital Platforms"\*\* (Focus: User experience, speed, and sales growth).

\- \*\*From "Automation"\*\* $\\rightarrow$ \*\*"Workflow Automation \& Optimization"\*\* (Focus: Reducing overhead and eliminating manual tasks).

\- \*\*From "Software Development"\*\* $\\rightarrow$ \*\*"Custom Business Infrastructure"\*\* (Focus: Scalable software tailored to unique operational needs).



\### D. Case Studies (The Proof)

Restructure the portfolio/case studies component. Every project must follow this pattern:

1\. \*\*The Challenge:\*\* What was the business pain?

2\. \*\*The Solution:\*\* What digital tool/platform did we build?

\*3. \*\*The Impact (Crucial):\*\* Use metrics (e.g., "Reduced processing time by 40%", "Increased lead capture by 25%").



\### E. Final CTA (The Lead Magnet)

Replace the standard footer contact with a high-value offer:

"Ready to automate your growth? Let's audit your current processes for free."



\## 4. Technical Instructions for Claude Code

1\. \*\*Analyze existing components:\*\* Identify the Hero, Services, and Contact components in the codebase.

2\. \*\*Rewrite Copy:\*\* Apply the new "Strategic Partner" messaging across all text elements.

3\. \*\*Update UI/UX Hierarchy:\*\* 

&#x20;   - Increase visual emphasis on the "Impact/Results" in case studies.

&#x20;   - Ensure the "Pain Points" section uses high-contrast typography to grab attention.

4\. \*\*Content Injection:\*\* If a "Problem/Pain Points" section does not exist, create a new section component with modern, clean styling consistent with the current design system.

5\. \*\*Maintain Design System:\*\* Do not change the brand colors or fundamental CSS architecture; only improve the information architecture and copywriting.



\## 5. Deliverables

\- Updated `Hero` component.

\- New `PainPoints` component.

\- Refactored `Services` component.

\- Updated `CaseStudies` component structure.

\- Updated `Contact/CTA` components.
